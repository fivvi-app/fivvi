# Shadow-day guide

**Purpose:** watch one real working day of the pilot gym, so that we design for what actually happens and not for what people remember happening. Covers `ROADMAP.md §4.1`: shadow day, real artefacts, inventory.

**You** = the product owner running the session. **Gym owner** = the pilot gym's owner (also a PT; code **T1**). Other trainers are **T2, T3, …** and clients are **Klant A, Klant B, …** (see [README: no personal data](README.md#no-personal-data-about-clients-hard-rule)).

## 1. Before the day

- [ ] Pick a **typical, busy day**, preferably one that has private sessions, at least one group class and an evening block. Avoid holiday weeks.
- [ ] The gym owner has told the trainers working that day that you will be there.
- [ ] Before each private session, the trainer asks the client whether you may watch. If the client says no, you wait outside and only note start and end times.
- [ ] Agree that you will **not photograph screens or paper that show client data**. Don't ask for files; if something matters, ask the gym owner to describe it ([`import-checklist.md` §1](import-checklist.md#1-before-anything-is-shared)).
- [ ] Bring the printed timing log (§4), this guide, [`phone-inventory.md`](phone-inventory.md) forms, a stopwatch and spare paper.
- [ ] Book 45 minutes with the gym owner **after** the day (or the next morning) for the interview (§5).

**Ground rules for you**

1. **Observe, don't help.** Don't suggest better ways of working and don't mention features. You want to see today's workflow, not change it.
2. **Ask "why" later.** During a session only note what happens. Keep questions for gaps between sessions or for the interview.
3. **Log every switch.** Every time someone picks up a tool (phone, laptop, paper) for admin, start a row in the timing log.
4. **Watch for workarounds.** Double entry, searching, copying between tools, memory tricks, "I'll do it tonight".

## 2. What to observe per activity

Use the activity codes below in the timing log. **Codes P, B, L, N, A, O and C are the admin the MVP should reduce. Code X is admin outside the MVP (payroll, marketing, bookkeeping).** Record X too, but it is kept out of the admin-time target (see [`baseline-sheet.md`](baseline-sheet.md#admin-time-scope)).

| Code | Activity | Activiteit |
|---|---|---|
| **P** | Scheduling: making and changing the timetable, private sessions, substitutions | Planning |
| **B** | Booking: handling booking requests, cancellations, capacity, messages about appointments | Boekingen |
| **L** | Workout logging and programming: writing programmes, logging sets, looking up previous values | Trainingen loggen |
| **N** | Notes about clients and sessions | Notities |
| **A** | Attendance and no-shows | Aanwezigheid |
| **O** | Open Gym: deciding on and announcing free gym time | Open Gym |
| **C** | Commerce: invoices, payments, memberships and session packs | Facturen en betalingen |
| **X** | Other admin outside the MVP (payroll, marketing, bookkeeping) | Overige administratie |

### P — Scheduling / Planning

Look for:
- [ ] Where the **master timetable** lives (Excel? calendar? whiteboard?) and who can change it.
- [ ] How a **new private session** is planned: who proposes a time, how the trainer's availability is checked, where it ends up.
- [ ] How a **reschedule** happens, and how many tools are touched for one change.
- [ ] How **trainer substitution** for a group class works (sick trainer): who finds a replacement, how clients are told.
- [ ] How **locations** are handled: gym, client's home, outdoor, online, another gym (`PRODUCT.md §12`).
- [ ] Whether the gym owner has an overview of **all trainers' schedules** in one place, and whether trainers have one overview across several workplaces.
- [ ] Double bookings, clashes, or "wait, where am I at 10?" moments.

Things to ask the gym owner to describe (no files): the weekly class timetable, what a week in the calendar looks like, what columns the Excel planning sheet has.

### B — Booking / Boekingen

Look for:
- [ ] **Every channel** a booking arrives through: WhatsApp, Instagram DM, phone, SMS, email, in person, a booking tool. Tally each request you see.
- [ ] How **capacity** is checked ("are there still spots on Tuesday?").
- [ ] How a booking is **confirmed** to the client, and how long that takes from the first message.
- [ ] How **cancellations** and late cancellations are handled, and whether a waiting list exists (formal or informal).
- [ ] Whether any client prefers **English**, and how that is handled.
- [ ] Messages about appointments outside working hours (evenings, weekends).

### L — Workout logging / Trainingen loggen

Look for:
- [ ] Where **programmes** are written (Excel, notebook, app, head) and how they get to the session.
- [ ] How sets, reps and weights are **recorded during** the session: by whom, on what, one-handed or not, between sets or afterwards.
- [ ] How the trainer finds **what the client did last time**. Time it: from "let me check" to having the number.
- [ ] Whether difficulty or effort is recorded (e.g. easy / normal / hard, RPE).
- [ ] Which **exercise names** are used (Dutch, English, abbreviations, nicknames). Write down examples; they feed the exercise library and glossary.
- [ ] Phone use in the gym: where the phone sits, gloves or chalk, **Wi-Fi and mobile signal** on the gym floor (R12).

### N — Notes / Notities

Look for:
- [ ] **Every place** notes live: Excel column, phone notes app, WhatsApp to self, paper, memory.
- [ ] What kind of notes: preferences, injuries or health (**sensitive**, don't write the content down), progress, reminders.
- [ ] When notes are written (during, right after, end of day, never) and when they are read (before a session?).
- [ ] Whether notes are **shared** between trainers or with the client.
- [ ] Notes that got lost or couldn't be found.

### A — Attendance / Aanwezigheid (also observe)

- [ ] Is attendance for group classes recorded? How, and by whom?
- [ ] How is a **no-show** noticed and recorded? Does anything happen afterwards (message, charge, nothing)?
- [ ] Count today: booked, attended, cancelled, no-show per class and private session (numbers only).

### C — Invoices and payments / Facturen en betalingen (also observe)

- [ ] How is a payment or membership change handled: which app, by whom, how long? Count today's invoices made or edited by hand (numbers only; never copy names, amounts or IBANs).
- [ ] Which sessions or memberships are tracked outside the booking app (paper, Excel, Word)?
- [ ] Follow-up of failed or late payments: who, how, how often.

### O — Open Gym (also observe)

- [ ] Does Open Gym (free gym time with limited capacity) exist today? How is it offered and announced?
- [ ] Any client questions like "can I come in to train on my own on Friday?". Tally them.
- [ ] Moments when the gym owner is at the gym anyway with capacity to spare (`PRODUCT.md §29`).

## 5. Interview questions

About 45 minutes with the gym owner, after the observed day. Use **section 5.10** for a shorter 15-minute talk with the other trainers.

**Tips:** ask open questions and let silences run. Ask for the **last time** something happened ("Vertel eens over de laatste keer dat…"), not for what usually happens. Don't propose solutions. Write answers on separate paper, numbered by question (e.g. *3.2*).

### 5.1 Context / Context

**1.1** Hoe ziet een normale week er voor jou uit? Wanneer train je privéklanten, wanneer geef je groepslessen?\
*What does a normal week look like for you? When do you train private clients, when do you teach group classes?*

**1.2** Hoeveel trainers werken er bij jou, en hoe is de verdeling tussen privétrainingen en groepslessen?\
*How many trainers work with you, and how are private sessions and group classes split between them?*

**1.3** Werken jouw trainers ook op andere plekken of met eigen klanten buiten deze gym?\
*Do your trainers also work elsewhere, or have their own clients outside this gym?*

**1.4** Hoeveel actieve klanten heb je ongeveer? Hoeveel daarvan komen privé, hoeveel alleen voor groepslessen?\
*Roughly how many active clients do you have? How many come for private training, how many only for group classes?*

### 5.2 Scheduling / Planning

**2.1** Vertel eens over de laatste keer dat je een nieuwe privétraining hebt ingepland. Wat deed je stap voor stap?\
*Tell me about the last time you scheduled a new private session. What did you do, step by step?*

**2.2** Waar staat het rooster "officieel"? Wat gebeurt er als het ergens anders anders staat?\
*Where does the schedule "officially" live? What happens when it says something different somewhere else?*

**2.3** Wat gebeurt er als een trainer ziek is en een les niet kan geven? Hoe regel je vervanging en hoe hoort de klant het?\
*What happens when a trainer is ill and can't teach a class? How do you arrange a substitute, and how do clients find out?*

**2.4** Hoe weet een trainer waar hij of zij moet zijn en met wie? Hoe weet jij wat al je trainers vandaag doen?\
*How does a trainer know where they need to be and with whom? How do you know what all your trainers are doing today?*

**2.5** Is er de afgelopen maand iets misgegaan met de planning, bijvoorbeeld een dubbele afspraak of iemand die voor niets kwam?\
*Did anything go wrong with the schedule in the past month, such as a double booking or someone showing up for nothing?*

### 5.3 Booking / Boekingen

**3.1** Via welke kanalen boeken klanten nu een les of training? Welk kanaal wordt het meest gebruikt?\
*Through which channels do clients book a class or session today? Which one is used most?*

**3.2** Vertel eens over de laatste boeking voor een groepsles. Hoe lang duurde het van het eerste bericht tot de bevestiging?\
*Tell me about the last booking for a group class. How long did it take from the first message to the confirmation?*

**3.3** Hoe weet je hoeveel plekken er nog vrij zijn in een les?\
*How do you know how many spots are still free in a class?*

**3.4** Hoe gaan afzeggingen nu? Wat vind je een te late afzegging?\
*How do cancellations work today? What do you count as a late cancellation?*

**3.5** Hoeveel berichten over afspraken krijg je buiten werktijd? Wat doet dat met je?\
*How many messages about appointments do you get outside working hours? How does that affect you?*

**3.6** Zijn er klanten die liever in het Engels communiceren? Hoeveel ongeveer?\
*Are there clients who prefer to communicate in English? Roughly how many?*

**3.7** Hoe zou je het vinden als klanten zelf boeken via een link of QR-code, alleen met naam en e-mailadres? Wat zou je daarbij zorgen baren?\
*How would you feel about clients booking themselves through a link or QR code, with only their name and email? What would worry you about that?*

### 5.4 Workout logging / Trainingen loggen

**4.1** Waar schrijf je trainingsschema's nu op, en hoe neem je ze mee naar de training?\
*Where do you write training programmes today, and how do you bring them to the session?*

**4.2** Wat noteer je tijdens een training? Op welk moment: tussen de sets, na de oefening, na de training?\
*What do you record during a session? At what moment: between sets, after the exercise, after the session?*

**4.3** Vertel eens over de laatste keer dat je wilde weten wat een klant vorige keer deed. Hoe vond je het terug, en hoe lang duurde dat?\
*Tell me about the last time you wanted to know what a client did last time. How did you find it, and how long did it take?*

**4.4** Op een schaal van 1 tot 5: hoe makkelijk is het nu om te zien wat een klant vorige keer deed? (1 = heel moeilijk, 5 = heel makkelijk)\
*On a scale of 1 to 5: how easy is it today to see what a client did last time? (1 = very hard, 5 = very easy)*\
→ Score: ____ *(baseline for §8 "previous performance is easy to access")*

**4.5** Welke oefeningnamen gebruik je: Nederlands, Engels of een mix? Heb je eigen oefeningen of eigen namen?\
*Which exercise names do you use: Dutch, English or a mix? Do you have your own exercises or your own names for them?*

**4.6** Werkt je telefoon goed op de trainingsvloer: wifi, bereik, met krijt of handschoenen?\
*Does your phone work well on the gym floor: Wi-Fi, signal, with chalk or gloves?*

### 5.5 Notes / Notities

**5.1** Waar bewaar je notities over klanten? Noem alle plekken, ook je hoofd.\
*Where do you keep notes about clients? Name every place, including your head.*

**5.2** Wat voor soort dingen schrijf je op? *(Don't write down the content, only the kind: preferences, injuries, progress, …)*\
*What kinds of things do you write down?*

**5.3** Moeten andere trainers jouw notities kunnen zien? Moeten klanten sommige notities kunnen zien?\
*Should other trainers be able to see your notes? Should clients be able to see some of them?*

**5.4** Is het wel eens gebeurd dat je een notitie niet meer kon vinden, of dat informatie verloren ging?\
*Has it ever happened that you couldn't find a note, or that information got lost?*

### 5.6 Attendance and no-shows / Aanwezigheid en niet verschenen

**6.1** Houd je bij wie er bij een les aanwezig was? Hoe?\
*Do you keep track of who attended a class? How?*

**6.2** Hoe vaak komt het voor dat iemand geboekt heeft maar niet komt opdagen, zonder af te zeggen? Hoe weet je dat?\
*How often does someone book but not show up, without cancelling? How do you know?*

**6.3** Wat doe je als iemand niet verschijnt?\
*What do you do when someone doesn't show up?*

### 5.7 Open Gym / Open Gym

**7.1** Bied je nu vrij trainen of Open Gym aan? Zo ja, hoe laat je klanten weten wanneer het kan?\
*Do you offer free training or Open Gym today? If so, how do you let clients know when it's available?*

**7.2** Hoe vaak vragen klanten of ze zelf mogen komen trainen? Hoeveel plekken zou je per keer kunnen aanbieden?\
*How often do clients ask whether they can come in to train on their own? How many spots could you offer each time?*

**7.3** Op welke momenten ben je sowieso in de gym en zou je Open Gym kunnen aanbieden?\
*At which times are you at the gym anyway and could offer Open Gym?*

### 5.8 Tools and admin time / Tools en administratietijd

**8.1** Welke programma's, apps en papieren gebruik je voor je administratie? Laten we ze samen opschrijven.\
*Which programs, apps and paper do you use for your admin? Let's write them down together.*

**8.2** Hoeveel uur per week denk je kwijt te zijn aan planning, boekingen, loggen, notities, facturen en betalingen? En aan andere administratie?\
*How many hours a week do you think you spend on scheduling, booking, logging, notes, invoices and payments? And on other admin?*\
→ Estimate MVP scope: ____ h/week · other admin: ____ h/week *(compare with the 7-day diary in the [baseline sheet](baseline-sheet.md))*

**8.3** Welke administratieve taak kost je de meeste energie, niet per se de meeste tijd?\
*Which admin task costs you the most energy, not necessarily the most time?*

**8.4** Op een schaal van 1 tot 5: hoe tevreden ben je met hoe het nu werkt? (1 = heel ontevreden, 5 = heel tevreden)\
*On a scale of 1 to 5: how satisfied are you with how things work today? (1 = very dissatisfied, 5 = very satisfied)*\
→ Score: ____

### 5.9 Closing / Afsluiting

**9.1** Als je morgen één ding aan je huidige manier van werken kon veranderen, wat zou dat zijn?\
*If you could change one thing about the way you work tomorrow, what would it be?*

**9.2** Wat moet absoluut blijven werken zoals het nu werkt?\
*What absolutely has to keep working the way it does now?*

**9.3** Wanneer zou je zeggen: dit nieuwe systeem is een succes? Wanneer zou je teruggaan naar Excel?\
*When would you say this new system is a success? When would you go back to Excel?*

**9.4** Is er iets dat ik vandaag niet heb gezien maar wel zou moeten weten?\
*Is there anything I didn't see today that I should know about?*

### 5.10 Short version for other trainers / Korte versie voor andere trainers

Takes about 15 minutes per trainer. Do the [phone inventory](phone-inventory.md) with them at the same time.

**10.1** Hoe weet je elke dag waar je moet zijn en met wie?\
*How do you know every day where you need to be and with whom?*

**10.2** Hoe houd je bij wat je klanten tijdens een training doen, en hoe vind je terug wat ze vorige keer deden?\
*How do you keep track of what your clients do during a session, and how do you find what they did last time?*

**10.3** Op een schaal van 1 tot 5: hoe makkelijk is het nu om te zien wat een klant vorige keer deed?\
*On a scale of 1 to 5: how easy is it today to see what a client did last time?*\
→ Score: ____

**10.4** Waar bewaar je notities over klanten?\
*Where do you keep notes about clients?*

**10.5** Werk je ook op andere plekken of met eigen klanten? Hoe houd je die agenda's bij elkaar?\
*Do you also work elsewhere or with your own clients? How do you keep those schedules together?*

**10.6** Hoeveel tijd per week ben je kwijt aan planning, berichten over afspraken en bijhouden van trainingen?\
*How much time a week do you spend on scheduling, messages about appointments and keeping track of workouts?*\
→ Estimate: ____ h/week

**10.7** Wat is het meest irritante aan hoe het nu gaat?\
*What is the most annoying thing about how it works now?*
