## 2. Two roles: the gym or trainer, and us

At Trainer Hub, two parties are responsible for your data. Which one depends on the data.

**Do you book with a gym or trainer, or are you their client?** Then **that gym or trainer** is responsible for your data: your bookings, attendance, workouts and the notes your trainer keeps about you. They decide which data they record and why. We only process that data on their behalf, as a **processor**, under a processor agreement with them.

- If you want to know what a gym has recorded about you, or want something changed or deleted, contact **that gym or trainer** first. Their privacy notice applies to that data.
- If you reach us instead, we forward your request within 5 working days and help them handle it.

**We are responsible ourselves** for a few things:

- your Trainer Hub account and signing in;
- the security of the platform;
- product statistics using pseudonymous IDs;
- our relationship with the gyms and trainers that use Trainer Hub.

This privacy policy covers that data.

## 3. Which data do we process, and why?

### 3.1 When we are responsible

| What | Which data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your account and signing in** | Name, email address, language preference (Dutch or English), hashed sign-in codes and links, session data, app and device version | So you can sign in without a password and use Trainer Hub in your own language | Performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Security and abuse prevention** | IP address, browser and device data, hashed email address, timestamps | To stop anyone from blocking classes with fake bookings or attacking the platform | Legitimate interest: a secure, reliable service (art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Product statistics** | Which features are used, with pseudonymous user and workspace IDs. These are personal data. **No** names, email addresses, booking, workout or note content | To improve Trainer Hub | Legitimate interest: product improvement (art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Contact with the gyms and trainers that use Trainer Hub** | Name, email address, organization, messages | Support, agreements, important notices about the service | Performance of a contract; legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(b) and (f)) |
| **Legal obligations** | Contract and (later) invoice data | Bookkeeping and retention obligations | Legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)) |

### 3.2 When we process on behalf of a gym or trainer

We process this data only on behalf of the gym or trainer:

- **Booking:** your name, email address and language, the class or Open Gym slot you choose, and the status of your booking. To book, you only need your name and email address. We don't ask for a phone number or a password.
- **Client data:** your profile at that gym, your preferred location, your appointments and your attendance.
- **Workouts:** exercises, sets, reps, weights and how hard it felt.
- **Notes:** your trainer's free-text notes. These may contain **health information**, for example about an injury. Notes are **visible to trainers only** by default. Your trainer can deliberately share a note with you.
- **Emails:** a sign-in link or code, confirmations, cancellations and changes, in the language you choose.

## 4. Cookies

Trainer Hub uses **only functional cookies and similar storage**, for example to keep you signed in and remember your language choice. These don't need your consent.

- We use **no** advertising or tracking cookies.
- Our product statistics work **without cookies**.
- On the booking form we use **Cloudflare Turnstile** to stop bots. Turnstile looks at technical data from your browser, such as your IP address and browser type. Cloudflare also uses that data itself, as a controller, to improve its bot detection. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 5. Who do we share data with?

- We **don't sell** your data or use it for advertising.
- We use a number of service providers, for example for hosting, the database, email and error reports. They process data only on our instructions, and we have data protection agreements with each of them. The current list, with purpose and location, is at **{{link to sub-processors}}**.
- A gym or trainer only sees data within their own environment. Other gyms can't see that you are also their client.
- We only give data to others when the law requires it, for example under a court order.

Transfers require an applicable safeguard, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 7. How long do we keep your data?

We don't keep data longer than necessary. The main periods:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| A booking you didn't confirm (expired) | About 48 hours at most, then deleted |
| Sign-in links and codes | Valid for 15 minutes; deleted after 48 hours at most |
| Bookings and client data at a gym | For as long as you are a client; by default anonymized or put up for review 24 months after your last activity. The gym can delete sooner |
| Data deleted by the gym | 30 days in the trash, then permanently deleted |
| Your account | Until you delete it; after 24 months of inactivity you get a warning, and it's deleted 30 days later |
| Security data (such as IP addresses) | 24 hours up to 30 days at most |
| Product statistics | 12 months |
| Backups | 30 days |

The full retention policy is at **{{link to retention policy}}**.

## 8. How do we protect your data?

We take appropriate measures, including:

- encrypted connections and encrypted storage;
- sign-in without a password, using single-use, short-lived codes;
- strict separation between gyms;
- role-based access;
- notes that only trainers see by default, with a log of who views them;
- regular backups.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- have processing restricted;
- take your data with you (portability);
- object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdraw your consent, where we process on the basis of consent.

**How:**

- For data held by a gym or trainer: contact them (see §2).
- For your account and the other data in §3.1: email {{privacy email address}}. You can also delete your account yourself in the app or on the website.

We respond **within one month**. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example with a code we send to your email address.

**Complaint?** Tell us first and we'll look for a solution together. You always have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
