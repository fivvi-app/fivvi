# Fivvi Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · 8 November 2026.** This is a translation of [`privacy-policy.nl.md`](privacy-policy.nl.md); if the two differ, the Dutch text prevails.

Fivvi (fivvi.app) is a platform that personal trainers and gyms use to manage their calendar, bookings, clients, memberships and credits, payments, workouts and notes. Trainers and owners use Fivvi Pro (iPhone, Android) and the website, clients use the app Fivvi or the website. This privacy policy explains which personal data we process, why, for how long, and what rights you have.

Fivvi is being built right now, together with our first gym. What follows about payments, workouts and notes applies once those parts are available.

## 2. Two roles: the gym or trainer, and us

At Fivvi, two parties are responsible for your data. Which one depends on the data. When you pay, a third party joins: Mollie.

**Are you a client of a gym, studio or trainer?** Then **that gym, studio or trainer** (the "studio") is responsible for your data: your request to join, bookings, attendance, memberships and credits, invoices, workouts and the notes your trainer keeps about you. The studio decides which data it records and why. We only process that data on the studio's behalf, as a **processor**, under a processor agreement with it.

- If you want to know what a studio has recorded about you, or want something changed or deleted, contact **the studio** first. Its privacy notice applies to that data.
- If you reach us instead, we forward your request within 5 working days and help the studio handle it.

**We are responsible ourselves** for a few things:

- your Fivvi account and signing in;
- the security of the platform;
- error reports and product statistics using pseudonymous IDs;
- questions to us and demo requests through the website;
- our relationship with the studios that use Fivvi.

This privacy policy covers that data.

**Mollie has a role of its own.** Paying your studio goes through Mollie, once that is available in Fivvi. Your studio has its own Mollie account and receives your payment itself. We don't accept money and don't hold clients' money. Mollie B.V. (Amsterdam) processes your payment details, such as your bank and account number and your direct debit mandate, as an **independent controller**, under its own terms. Read [Mollie's privacy statement](https://www.mollie.com/legal/privacy). We send Mollie only what a payment needs: your name, email address, the amount, a description without health or note information, and our own IDs. We only keep references, amounts, statuses and a masked account number, and no full IBAN.

## 3. Which data do we process, and why?

### 3.1 When we are responsible

| What | Which data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your account and signing in** | Name, email address, language preference (Dutch or English), hashed sign-in codes and links, session data, app and device version. We also send the email with your sign-in code or link | So you can sign in without a password and use Fivvi in your own language | Performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Security and abuse prevention** | IP address (only as a keyed value for limits per network), browser and device data, hashed email address, timestamps | To stop anyone from attacking the platform or flooding it with fake accounts and fake requests | Legitimate interest: a secure, reliable service (art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Error reports** | Technical data when something goes wrong or the app crashes: the page or route, app and operating system version, the device model (not the name of your device), a pseudonymous user ID or, while nobody is signed in, a random ID per installation. **No** names, email addresses, booking, workout or note content | To find and fix errors | Legitimate interest: a working service (art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Product statistics** | Which features are used and which pages of the web app are opened (the path without parameters), with pseudonymous user and workspace IDs. These are personal data. **No** names, email addresses, booking, workout or note content | To improve Fivvi | Legitimate interest: product improvement (art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Asking for a demo on the website** | Your name, email address, the kind of business (gym, studio or personal trainer), the number of trainers and, if you fill it in, your message | To answer your request and arrange a demo | Steps at your request before entering into a contract (art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Questions and privacy requests by email or phone** | Your name, email address or phone number and what you tell us | To answer you and handle your request | Legitimate interest: answering questions (art. 6(1)(f)); legal obligation for privacy requests (point (c)) |
| **Contact with studios that use Fivvi** | Name, email address, organization, messages | Support, agreements, important notices about the service | Performance of a contract; legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(b) and (f)) |
| **Legal obligations** | Contract data and, if we start charging, invoice data | Bookkeeping and retention obligations | Legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)) |

**The demo request in detail.** The form on the website sends your answers as **one email to our support address**. We don't store them in Fivvi's database, we log nothing about you, and you get no confirmation email, so the form can't be used to send mail to someone else. A hidden field keeps bots out, and a limit per network (a keyed value, not an IP address, for at most one hour) stops the inbox from filling up. Who processes the request? Our email service **Lettermint** sends the email, and our mailbox at **Zoho** receives and keeps it. The website itself runs at Railway (see §5). We keep the email in our mailbox for as long as we need it to answer you, and then at most 24 months after the last contact (§7). You can ask us to delete it sooner.

### 3.2 When we process on behalf of a studio

We process this data only on behalf of the studio:

- **Joining a studio:** your name, email address and language, your request to join through the studio's link or QR code, or the invitation the studio sends you, and whether the studio has approved you. An account without a studio shows nothing of any studio.
- **Client data and calendar:** your profile at that studio, your preferred location, your bookings and cancellations, your place on a waitlist and your attendance.
- **Memberships, credits and invoices (once available):** your membership or credit pack, your credit balance, the status of your payments and your invoices. The payment itself goes through Mollie (§2).
- **Workouts (once available):** exercises, sets, reps, weights and how hard it felt.
- **Notes (once available):** your trainer's free-text notes. These may contain **health information**, for example about an injury. Notes are **visible to trainers only** by default. Your trainer can deliberately share a note with you.
- **Emails from the studio through Fivvi:** invitations, confirmations, cancellations and changes, waitlist and membership notices, in the language you choose.

## 4. Cookies

Fivvi uses **only functional cookies**, in the web app after you sign in. These don't need your consent:

- a sign-in cookie, to keep you signed in (30 days after you last use it);
- a language cookie, to remember your language choice (1 year);
- a short language choice on the sign-in page (1 day);
- a cookie with the last studio you opened (1 year). It holds no personal data.

The public website (fivvi.app, the pages about Fivvi, pricing, help and these texts) sets **no cookies**, doesn't count page views and doesn't let any third party watch.

- We use **no** advertising or tracking cookies.
- Our product statistics in the web app work **without cookies**. Nothing stays behind in your browser.
- The apps Fivvi Pro and Fivvi use no advertising or analytics SDK. They only report errors and crashes (see §3.1).

## 5. Who do we share data with?

- We **don't sell** your data or use it for advertising.
- We use a number of service providers for hosting and the database (Railway), email (Lettermint), error reports (Sentry), product statistics (PostHog) and our own mailbox (Zoho). They process data only on our instructions, and we have data protection agreements with each of them. The current list, with purpose and location, is at **https://fivvi.app/en/sub-processors**.
- **Mollie** is not a service provider of ours for your payment. Mollie works for your studio and for itself (§2).
- A studio only sees data within its own environment. Other studios can't see that you are also their client.
- If you get the app from the App Store or Google Play, Apple and Google process data about your download and your store account under their own terms. We receive no personal data from that.
- We only give data to others when the law requires it, for example under a court order.

## 6. Where is your data stored?

- The app and PostgreSQL database run on Railway in Amsterdam. We keep backups for 30 days at most, in the EU.
- Email goes through Lettermint, a Dutch company that processes email data only in Amsterdam and Germany. Our mailbox is at Zoho, in a European data centre.
- Error reports and product statistics go to Sentry and PostHog, both in their EU environment in Frankfurt.
- Railway, Sentry and PostHog are US companies, and Railway states in its DPA that primary processing takes place in the United States. Zoho is an international group. Transfers outside the EEA require an applicable safeguard, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. These apply here.

## 7. How long do we keep your data?

We don't keep data longer than necessary. The main periods:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Sign-in links and codes | Valid for 15 minutes; deleted after 48 hours at most |
| Bookings and client data at a studio | For as long as you are a client; by default anonymized or put up for review 24 months after your last activity. The studio can delete sooner |
| Data deleted by the studio | 30 days in the trash, then permanently deleted |
| Your account | Until you have it deleted; after 24 months of inactivity you get a warning, and it's deleted 30 days later |
| Security data (such as the values for limits) | 24 hours up to 30 days at most |
| Error reports | 90 days at most |
| Product statistics | 12 months |
| Emails at our email service (content, recipient, delivery status) | 28 days; statistics about delivery 90 days. We can't set that shorter at the provider |
| Demo requests, questions and privacy requests in our mailbox | Up to 24 months after the last contact; a privacy request and our answer are kept for as long as we need to show that we handled it |
| Invoices (once we start charging or studios issue invoices through Fivvi) | 7 years (tax retention obligation) |
| Backups | 30 days |

Data that Mollie processes for itself is kept by Mollie under its own periods.

## 8. How do we protect your data?

We take appropriate measures, including:

- encrypted connections and encrypted storage;
- sign-in without a password, using single-use, short-lived codes;
- strict separation between studios;
- role-based access;
- notes that only trainers see by default, with a log of who views them;
- regular backups.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- have processing restricted;
- take your data with you (portability);
- object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdraw your consent, where we process on the basis of consent.

**How:**

- For data held by a studio: contact it (see §2).
- For your account and the other data in §3.1: email **privacy@fivvi.app**. To have your account deleted, email privacy@fivvi.app from the address of your account with the subject "Delete account". We do that within one month.
- For data held by Mollie: contact Mollie.

We respond **within one month**. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example with a code we send to your email address.

**Complaint?** Tell us first and we'll look for a solution together. You always have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## 10. Children

You can create a Fivvi account from the age of **16**. Younger children have no account of their own: a parent or guardian arranges it with the studio for them.

## 11. Automated decisions

We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 12. Changes

We may update this privacy policy, for example when Fivvi gets new features. We announce important changes in advance by email or in the app. The date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
